How this works

  1. Plug the device into your computer with a good USB data cable (not a charge-only cable).
  2. Pick what you're flashing and which board you have.
  3. Click Connect & Flash. Your browser will show a port picker — usually one entry labelled something like USB JTAG/serial or CP210x or CH340.
  4. Pick that port and wait. The firmware will erase, write, and verify. Takes 20–60 seconds.
  5. When done, unplug and re-plug the device to boot the new firmware.

If a device won't connect

  1. Hold the BOOT button while plugging in the USB cable (this forces flash mode).
  2. Try a different USB cable — many "charging" cables have no data lines.
  3. On XIAO boards: press RESET while holding BOOT, then release RESET first.

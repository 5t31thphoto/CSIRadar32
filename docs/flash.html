Flash flow

  1. Plug in with a USB data cable (not charge-only).
  2. Pick device role & board above.
  3. Click Connect & Flash, pick the port in the browser dialog.
  4. Wait 20-60 s. When done, unplug + re-plug to boot.

If a device won't connect

  1. Hold BOOT while plugging in to force flash mode.
  2. Try a different USB cable — many "charging" cables have no data lines.
  3. XIAO: hold BOOT + tap RESET, release RESET first.

How to place the boards

Tripwire (1 beacon)

  • Receiver and beacon 1–5 m apart, at similar height.
  • Anything crossing the line between them will register.
  • Skip walk-cal — tripwire mode goes straight from empty-room baseline to running.

Full mode (2 receivers + 3 beacons)

  • Beacons roughly triangular, similar height, 1–3 m spacing between them.
  • Receivers on a 6 cm bar — anchor stays there for cal, probe walks with you.
  • During cal you'll visit each beacon, the centroid, three edge midpoints, and a point behind the receiver bar. The device tells you what to do at each stop.
